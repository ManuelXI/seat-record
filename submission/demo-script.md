# Seat Record: demo script (about 4 minutes)

All people, clients and figures are fictional. The scripted entry shows an illustrative response written to show what the model returns; the model is not called during the demo.
Each handoff is a role switch: click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and pick the next person.

## Before recording

- Press **Reset demo data** in the sidebar, then open the switcher and choose **Sign out**, so you start on the sign-in page with clean data.
- Rehearse once end to end.

## 0:00 to 0:30 The problem

**Screen:** The landing page, for two seconds: the before-and-after card. Click **Try the demo** and sign in as **Efua**. Click **My profile** and set the toggle to **Before Seat Record**. The Experience section reads "Worked on client systems (confidential). Developed and maintained features."

**Say:** "This is Efua. She's spent fourteen months inside a bank's risk team, and she writes this profile herself. Everything she built lives in the client's systems, under NDA, so she doesn't know what she's allowed to say, and nobody confirms it. Kofi, a contractor, has it worse: no manager, no review, no record at all."

**Say:** "A dated client approval, signed by the employer, is the one credential a model can't mint. Seat Record makes it."

## 0:30 to 1:30 Efua logs her work

**Screen:** **My seat log**. Earlier entries, the extension checkpoint approved in February, the monthly reminder on.

**Do:** Click **Log your work**. Type, or click the **Client system name** example: "I rebuilt their overnight VaR batch so it stopped failing on the Aurora ledger feed, and set up contract tests for the three upstream teams."

**Show:** "Aurora" turns red before anything is sent. Tap the **upstream** suggestion; the sentence now reads "the upstream ledger feed". "The suggestions are a fixed list on purpose: nothing reaches a model until the screen is clear." "This check runs in her browser. Raw client text never leaves her laptop."

**Show:** Click **Check and draft lines**. The label at the top says this is an illustrative response: the model is not called in the demo. It flags "VaR" with a reason; click **Use "risk"**. The line "Eliminated all failures" is greyed: those words are not in her entry. Edit it to "Removed recurring failures in an overnight process". Click **Approve**.

## 1:30 to 2:00 Ama, her manager, opens a checkpoint

**Do:** Switch to **Ama**.

**Show:** "Needs attention" lists Efua. Open her **Engagement**. Point out: Ama sees status and what the client approved, and a count of lines in Efua's log she cannot read. "Managers never see a worker's unshared entries, and there is no send button here."

**Do:** Reason **Client lead is leaving**, note "Your client lead moves on next month", click **Open checkpoint**.

## 2:00 to 2:50 Efua sends, the client approves

**Do:** Switch to **Efua**. The banner shows Ama's note. Click **Review lines**. Everything unapproved is ticked. Untick the dashboard line and keep the incident review. Click **Approve and send**, then **Open client email (mock)**.

**Say:** "We never send the client a new message. The link rides on an email they already get. Client leads have no account."

**Do:** As the client lead: approve most lines, edit one, decline one. Optionally add a short testimonial. Click **Confirm**. "Signed by Bonarda Works", with the signature shown.

## 2:50 to 3:30 The payoff

**Do:** Click **Back to Efua's profile**, toggle **With Seat Record**: approved lines with tier chips. Click **Seat history**: every signed record shows "Signature valid". Click **Try editing a word**: "Signature invalid". Undo. "Her record is private. She can turn on an unlisted link to share it, and replace it any time."

**Do:** Switch to **Ama**, click **Find people**, and click the example "Java developer who has handled Kafka failures and run incident reviews". Switch the filter to **Evidence for every requirement**: Efua is the only one, with a client-approved quote beside each requirement, including the incident review she got approved a minute ago. "No score, no ranking. Just the evidence, quoted, and the manager decides."

**Say:** "Kofi gets the same record, and exports his copy when he leaves. It works for employees, contractors and freelancers alike."

## 3:30 to 4:00 Close

**Screen:** **How it works** (real-versus-mocked table), then **Evaluation**.

**Say:** "The browser check, grounding, tiers and signing are real and tested. Emails and the placement list are mocked, and in this demo the model's responses are illustrative, labelled on screen. The model never scores or ranks anyone. It does one job a form can't: it makes a true sentence safe to say."

## If a judge asks what else the screen catches

On **Log your work**, click the other examples: a colleague's name, figures and money, a ticket and hostname. Each is caught in the browser before anything is sent.
